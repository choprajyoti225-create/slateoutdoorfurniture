The calendar

Most of this is timing,not skill

Doing the right job in the wrong month is most of what goes wrong. Sealer on damp timber peels. Foam dried in October never dries. A rust blister found in March has already had five months to spread.

The two dates that matter

If you only ever do two things: a long pass on the first dry mild day of spring, and a short one on a dry day in late autumn before the covers go on. Everything else on this page is optional detail hanging off those two hours.
